About Us

What is FundiLink?

We built FundiLink because finding a casual worker in Kenya is still done through WhatsApp groups and word of mouth. There had to be a better way.

Our platform lets employers post jobs and workers apply — all matched by location so nobody wastes time travelling across the city.
